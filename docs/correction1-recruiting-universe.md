# Correction 1: recruiting universe and first discovery

This correction widens recall inside the existing discovery and classification design. It does not change fit, eligibility, ranking, or role-family weights other than adding Marketing at 5.

Marketing is its own family, with subfamily `MARKETING_GENERAL`. Product marketing stays product marketing. A job is not marketing just because the description says the word marketing.

Technology strategy is `TECHNOLOGY_STRATEGY` under strategy and transformation. Technology consulting stays consulting when the work is client advisory.

MBA summer-associate titles are searched as specific phrases, and the internship gate accepts those controlled titles. The word Associate by itself is not an internship.

The first `discovery_family`, `discovery_bucket`, and `discovery_query` stored for a source and external id stay in place. A later search can fill a blank field. A different source remains a different job.
